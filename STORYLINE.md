# Pallimustus: The Fractured Veil — Storyline

## Premise
Jason Asano, an outworlder stranded in the world of Pallimustus, reaches the frontier city of Greenstone. The region is tense: monsters roam beyond the walls, Adventure Society patrols are stretched thin, and an astral rift has torn open near the old eastern road. Its unstable magic threatens to spill astral creatures into Greenstone territory.

## Prologue — Outworlder in Greenstone
- **Setting:** Greenstone Outskirts, at dusk.
- **Situation:** Jason regroups with Maria, a Greenstone guard, and Rocky, his magic-gorilla companion. The local wilds are dangerous, but the new rift is worse than ordinary monster activity.
- **Goal:** Investigate the disturbance and secure the frontier.

## Chapter 1 — The Sundered Eastern Road
- **Region:** Greenstone Outskirts.
- **Key events:**
  - Jason explores the trailhead and caravan road outside Greenstone.
  - A Rift-Touched Hound stalks the player, warped by astral energy.
  - Defeating the hound yields a **Faint Shadow Essence**, the first loot-based essence progression.
- **Story beat:** The hound proves the rift is already changing local monsters.

## Chapter 2 — The Frontier Regions
The map opens into six regions, each with its own danger, landmarks, and atmosphere.

1. **Greenstone Outskirts** — Caravan roads, patrols, and the first signs of corruption.
2. **The Oldwoods** — Ancient forest with scout camps, moss shrines, and stalking beasts.
3. **Ashfen Marsh** — Sunken fenland, toxic miasma, leechbeasts, and old spirit markers.
4. **Sundered Ruins** — Broken kingdom remnants where the Astral Rift anchors itself.
5. **Saltshard Coast** — Wind-battered cliffs, shipwrecks, and cold sea air.
6. **Ironridge Highlands** — High passes, wyrm country, and hunter lookouts.

- **Gameplay/story purpose:** Players gather supplies, rest at camps, restore aura at shrines, recover loot from ruins/wrecks, and fight region monsters.
- **Regional enemies:** Oldwoods Stalker, Ashfen Leechbeast, Ruin Wraith, Ironridge Wyrm.

## Chapter 3 — The Astral Rift
- **Region:** Sundered Ruins / Astral Kingdom.
- **Key events:**
  - Jason locates the astral rift in the ruins.
  - Entering the rift transports him to the Astral Kingdom, a fractured reality of unstable energy.
  - Astral wraiths guard the rift anchor and must be defeated before escape is possible.
- **Story beat:** The astral realm is not just another battlefield; it is a pressure point between worlds.

## Chapter 4 — The Veil Restored
- **Key events:**
  - After defeating the astral creatures, Jason escapes the rift.
  - He claims the **Veil-Touched Essence** as quest reward/loot progression.
  - The rift stabilizes, but the frontier remains dangerous.
- **Outcome:** Greenstone gains breathing room, while Jason’s essence array begins to take shape.

## Secret Chapter — The Sandwich Confluence (Preview/Test Secret)
- **Secret essences:** Peanut Butter, Jam, Bread, and **Sandwich** (confluence).
- **Discovery:** Intended as a hidden, overpowered preview/test set, accessible through the preview console.
- **Theme:** A playful, absurdly powerful confluence that contrasts with the grim frontier tone.
- **Gameplay:** Equipping the full set unlocks the Sandwich supernova ability — massively overpowered for testing.

## Ongoing Hooks
- Adventure Society politics and restricted essences.
- Deeper astral incursions beyond the first rift.
- Factions around Greenstone: guards, guilds, hunters, and outworlders.
- Expansion of essence combinations, confluences, and rank progression.
